## 1. Core Concepts & Instruments

| Term / Abbr | Full form | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **Asset** | — | Anything of value you own that can produce income or be sold (stocks, bonds, property, cash). |
| **Asset Class** | — | A group of investments with similar behaviour — Equity, Fixed Income, Cash, Commodities, Real Estate, Alternatives. |
| **Bond** | — | A debt instrument: you lend money to an issuer who pays periodic interest (coupon) and repays principal at maturity. |
| **Commodity** | — | A basic physical good that is traded — gold, oil, wheat, copper. |
| **Derivative** | — | A contract whose value is *derived* from an underlying asset (options, futures, swaps). |
| **Equity** | — | Ownership in a company; the residual value after liabilities. For a stock investor, "equity" = shares. |
| **ETF** | Exchange-Traded Fund | A fund that trades on an exchange like a stock, usually tracking an index. |
| **FD** | Fixed Deposit | A bank deposit locked for a fixed term at a fixed interest rate **(IN)**. |
| **Forex / FX** | Foreign Exchange | The market for trading currencies. |
| **Fund** | — | A pooled vehicle that collects money from many investors to invest collectively (mutual fund, hedge fund, etc.). |
| **Index** | — | A basket of securities that measures a market or segment (e.g. Nifty 50, S&P 500). |
| **Liability** | — | What you owe — debts and obligations. |
| **Liquidity** | — | How easily an asset can be bought/sold without moving its price. Cash is the most liquid. |
| **MF** | Mutual Fund | A professionally managed pooled fund investing in stocks/bonds on behalf of investors. |
| **Money Market** | — | Market for very short-term, low-risk debt (T-bills, commercial paper). |
| **Portfolio** | — | The full collection of investments held by a person or fund. |
| **Principal** | — | The original amount invested or borrowed, excluding interest. |
| **Security** | — | A tradable financial instrument (stock, bond, derivative). |
| **Share / Stock** | — | A unit of ownership in a company. "Stock" is generic; a "share" is one specific unit. |
| **Yield** | — | The income return on an investment, expressed as a % of price (e.g. dividend yield, bond yield). |

| **IPO** | Initial Public Offering | A company's first sale of shares to the public. |
| **Large / Mid / Small Cap** | Market Capitalisation tiers | Size buckets by market value — large (biggest, safest), mid, small (riskier, higher growth). |

| **OHLC** | Open, High, Low, Close | The four key prices of a trading period. |
| **Penny Stock** | — | Very low-priced, high-risk, often illiquid shares. |

| Term / Abbr | Full form | Definition |
|---|---|---|

| **AUM** | Assets Under Management | Total market value of assets a fund/manager oversees. |
| **Active Fund** | — | A fund where a manager picks holdings aiming to beat a benchmark. |
| **CAGR** | Compound Annual Growth Rate | The smoothed annual return over a period (see [[#6. Risk & Performance Metrics]]). |
| **Direct vs Regular Plan** | — | Direct = bought from AMC, no distributor commission (lower expense); Regular = via distributor **(IN)**. |

| **Index Fund** | — | A passive fund that replicates an index (low cost). |
| **NAV** | Net Asset Value | Per-unit value of a fund = (assets − liabilities) / units. |
| **NFO** | New Fund Offer | The launch subscription period of a new MF scheme. |
| **Passive Fund** | — | A fund that tracks an index rather than picking stocks. |
| **SIP** | Systematic Investment Plan | Investing a fixed amount at regular intervals (rupee-cost averaging) **(IN)**. |
| **STP** | Systematic Transfer Plan | Auto-transfer of money from one fund to another periodically **(IN)**. |
| **SWP** | Systematic Withdrawal Plan | Auto-withdrawal of a fixed amount from a fund periodically. |
| **Tracking Error** | — | How much a fund's return deviates from its benchmark index. |
| **XIRR** | Extended Internal Rate of Return | Annualised return accounting for multiple cash flows at irregular dates (used for SIPs). |

## 4. Bonds & Fixed Income

| Term / Abbr | Full form | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **Accrued Interest** | — | Interest earned but not yet paid since the last coupon. |
| **Callable Bond** | — | Bond the issuer can redeem early. |
| **Convexity** | — | Curvature of the price–yield relationship; refines duration for large rate moves. |
| **Coupon** | — | The periodic interest a bond pays, as a % of face value. |
| **Credit Rating** | — | Agency assessment of default risk (AAA down to D). |
| **Credit Spread** | — | Extra yield over a risk-free bond to compensate for credit risk. |
| **Duration** | Macaulay / Modified | Sensitivity of a bond's price to interest-rate changes (in years / % per 1% rate move). |
| **Face / Par Value** | — | The principal repaid at maturity (e.g. ₹1,000). |
| **G-Sec** | Government Security | Bond issued by the government **(IN)**; sovereign, near risk-free. |
| **HY** | High Yield ("Junk") | Below-investment-grade bonds paying higher yield for higher risk. |
| **Investment Grade** | IG | Bonds rated BBB−/Baa3 or above; lower default risk. |
| **Maturity** | — | The date the bond's principal is repaid. |
| **T-Bill** | Treasury Bill | Short-term (≤1 yr) government debt sold at a discount, no coupon. |
| **YTM** | Yield to Maturity | Total annualised return if the bond is held to maturity. |
| **Yield Curve** | — | Plot of yields across maturities; inverted curve (short > long) often signals recession. |
| **Zero-Coupon Bond** | — | Pays no coupon; issued at a discount, redeemed at par. |

## 5. Derivatives — Options, Futures & the Greeks

| Term / Abbr | Full form | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **ATM / ITM / OTM** | At/In/Out of The Money | Option moneyness vs strike: ATM = strike ≈ spot; ITM = has intrinsic value; OTM = none. |
| **Call Option** | — | Right (not obligation) to **buy** the underlying at the strike by expiry. |
| **Put Option** | — | Right to **sell** the underlying at the strike by expiry. |
| **Contango / Backwardation** | — | Futures price above (contango) / below (backwardation) the spot price. |
| **Expiry** | — | The date a derivative contract settles. |
| **Forward** | — | An OTC (private) contract to buy/sell an asset at a set future price. |
| **Futures** | — | Standardised, exchange-traded forward contract. |
| **Hedge** | — | A position taken to offset risk in another position. |
| **Intrinsic vs Time Value** | — | Option price = intrinsic (in-the-money amount) + time value (premium for time/uncertainty). |
| **Leverage** | — | Using borrowed money / derivatives to amplify exposure (and risk). |
| **LEAPS** | Long-term Equity Anticipation Securities | Options with expiries over a year out. |
| **Lot Size** | — | Fixed quantity of the underlying per derivative contract. |
| **Margin** | — | Collateral posted to hold a leveraged/derivative position. |
| **Notional Value** | — | Total value the contract controls (lot size × price). |
| **Open Interest** | OI | Number of outstanding (unclosed) derivative contracts. |
| **Premium** | — | Price paid by an option buyer to the seller (writer). |
| **Spot Price** | — | The current market price of the underlying. |
| **Strike Price** | — | The pre-agreed price at which an option can be exercised. |
| **Swap** | — | An OTC contract to exchange cash flows (e.g. interest-rate swap, currency swap). |
| **Writer** | — | The seller of an option, who takes on the obligation. |

### The Option Greeks

| Greek | Measures | Plain meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **Delta (Δ)** | Sensitivity to underlying price | ₹ change in option price per ₹1 move in the underlying (0–1 calls, −1–0 puts). |
| **Gamma (Γ)** | Rate of change of Delta | How fast Delta moves; highest near ATM. |
| **Theta (Θ)** | Time decay | How much value the option loses per day as expiry nears. |
| **Vega (ν)** | Sensitivity to volatility | Price change per 1% change in implied volatility. |
| **Rho (ρ)** | Sensitivity to interest rates | Price change per 1% change in rates. |
| **IV** | Implied Volatility | Market's expected future volatility, back-solved from the option price. |

## 6. Risk & Performance Metrics

The "advanced" toolkit — how return is measured and adjusted for risk. Grouped A–Z.

| Metric | Full form | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| **Alpha (α)** | — | Excess return vs what the benchmark/market would predict; the manager's "skill" return. Positive α = outperformance. |
| **Annualised Return** | — | A return rescaled to a yearly rate for comparison. |
| **Beta (β)** | — | Sensitivity to the market. β=1 moves with the market; >1 more volatile; <1 less; negative = moves opposite. |
| **CAGR** | Compound Annual Growth Rate | Smoothed annual growth: `(End/Start)^(1/years) − 1`. Ignores volatility along the way. |
| **Calmar Ratio** | — | CAGR ÷ max drawdown; return per unit of worst-case loss. |
| **Correlation** | — | How two assets move together (−1 to +1); key for diversification. |
| **Downside Deviation** | — | Volatility of only negative returns (used in Sortino). |
| **Drawdown / Max Drawdown (MDD)** | — | Peak-to-trough fall; MDD is the worst such fall — a gut-check on pain. |
| **Expense-adjusted / Net Return** | — | Return after fees and costs. |
| **Information Ratio** | IR | Active return ÷ tracking error; consistency of beating the benchmark. |
| **IRR** | Internal Rate of Return | The discount rate that makes an investment's NPV zero; the effective annual return across cash flows. |
| **Jensen's Alpha** | — | Alpha derived from CAPM specifically. |
| **Kurtosis** | — | "Fat tails" — how often extreme returns occur vs a normal distribution. |
| **Max Drawdown** | MDD | See Drawdown. |
| **R-squared (R²)** | — | % of a fund's moves explained by its benchmark (0–100%); high R² makes Beta/Alpha more meaningful. |
| **Sharpe Ratio** | — | `(Return − Risk-free rate) ÷ Std Dev`. Return per unit of *total* risk. Higher = better risk-adjusted return. |
| **Skewness** | — | Asymmetry of returns; negative skew = occasional big losses. |
| **Sortino Ratio** | — | Like Sharpe but divides by *downside* deviation only — penalises harmful volatility, not upside. |
| **Standard Deviation (σ)** | — | Dispersion of returns around the average; the standard measure of volatility/risk. |
| **Treynor Ratio** | — | `(Return − Risk-free) ÷ Beta`. Return per unit of *market* (systematic) risk. |
| **VaR** | Value at Risk | The max expected loss over a period at a confidence level (e.g. 95% 1-day VaR). |
| **CVaR / Expected Shortfall** | Conditional VaR | Average loss *beyond* the VaR threshold — the tail severity. |
| **Volatility** | — | See Standard Deviation; often annualised. |

> **Risk types:** *Systematic risk* (market-wide, undiversifiable — captured by Beta) vs *Unsystematic risk* (company/sector-specific, diversifiable).

## 7. Valuation Ratios & Fundamental Analysis

| Ratio / Abbr | Full form | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| **Book Value / BVPS** | Book Value Per Share | Net assets (equity) per share; accounting value. |
| **Current Ratio** | — | Current assets ÷ current liabilities; short-term liquidity (>1 is healthier). |
| **D/E** | Debt-to-Equity | Total debt ÷ shareholder equity; leverage/solvency. |
| **Dividend Yield** | — | Annual dividend ÷ share price. |
| **DCF** | Discounted Cash Flow | Valuation by projecting future cash flows and discounting to present value. |
| **EPS** | Earnings Per Share | Net profit ÷ shares outstanding. |
| **EV** | Enterprise Value | Market cap + debt − cash; the "takeover" value of a firm. |
| **EV/EBITDA** | — | Enterprise value relative to operating earnings; capital-structure-neutral valuation. |
| **FCF** | Free Cash Flow | Operating cash flow − capex; cash available to investors. |
| **Intrinsic Value** | — | An asset's "true" worth from fundamentals, vs its market price. |
| **MOS** | Margin of Safety | Gap between intrinsic value and price; a value-investing buffer. |
| **P/B** | Price-to-Book | Share price ÷ book value per share. |
| **P/E** | Price-to-Earnings | Share price ÷ EPS; how much you pay per ₹1 of earnings. |
| **PEG** | P/E-to-Growth | P/E ÷ earnings growth rate; P/E adjusted for growth (≈1 seen as fair). |
| **P/S** | Price-to-Sales | Market cap ÷ revenue. |
| **Payout Ratio** | — | Dividends ÷ net income; share of profit paid out. |
| **Quick Ratio** | Acid-Test | (Current assets − inventory) ÷ current liabilities; stricter liquidity. |
| **ROA** | Return on Assets | Net income ÷ total assets; asset efficiency. |
| **ROCE** | Return on Capital Employed | EBIT ÷ capital employed; return on all capital (debt + equity). |
| **ROE** | Return on Equity | Net income ÷ shareholder equity; return on owners' money. |
| **ROIC** | Return on Invested Capital | After-tax operating profit ÷ invested capital. |

## 8. Financial Statements & Accounting

| Term / Abbr | Full form | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **Amortisation** | — | Spreading the cost of an intangible asset (or loan principal) over time. |
| **Balance Sheet** | — | Snapshot of assets = liabilities + equity at a point in time. |
| **CapEx** | Capital Expenditure | Spending on long-term assets (plant, equipment). |
| **COGS** | Cost of Goods Sold | Direct costs of producing goods sold. |
| **Cash Flow Statement** | — | Cash in/out across Operating, Investing, Financing activities. |
| **Depreciation** | — | Spreading the cost of a tangible asset over its useful life. |
| **EBIT** | Earnings Before Interest & Taxes | Operating profit. |
| **EBITDA** | EBIT + Depreciation & Amortisation | Proxy for operating cash earnings. |
| **GAAP / IFRS** | Accounting standards | US GAAP vs International Financial Reporting Standards. |
| **Goodwill** | — | Premium paid over fair value in an acquisition; an intangible asset. |
| **Gross / Operating / Net Margin** | — | Profit at each level ÷ revenue (gross → after COGS; operating → after opex; net → after everything). |
| **Income Statement / P&L** | Profit and Loss | Revenue, expenses and profit over a period. |
| **NPM** | Net Profit Margin | Net income ÷ revenue. |
| **OpEx** | Operating Expenses | Day-to-day running costs. |
| **Revenue / Top Line** | — | Total sales before costs. |
| **Net Income / Bottom Line** | — | Final profit after all costs and taxes. |
| **Working Capital** | — | Current assets − current liabilities; short-term operating funds. |

|---|---|---|

## 10. Trading, Orders & Market Microstructure

| Term / Abbr | Full form | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **Ask / Offer** | — | The lowest price a seller will accept. |
| **Bid** | — | The highest price a buyer will pay. |
| **Bid-Ask Spread** | — | Gap between bid and ask; a liquidity/cost measure. |
| **Brokerage** | — | Fee a broker charges per trade. |

## 11. Portfolio Management & Asset Allocation

| Term / Abbr | Full form | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **Asset Allocation** | — | Splitting a portfolio across asset classes (e.g. 60/40 equity/debt). |
| **CAPM** | Capital Asset Pricing Model | Expected return = risk-free + β × (market return − risk-free). |
| **Diversification** | — | Spreading investments to reduce unsystematic risk. |
| **Dollar-Cost / Rupee-Cost Averaging** | — | Investing fixed amounts regularly to average the buy price (see SIP). |
| **Efficient Frontier** | — | Set of portfolios giving max return for each risk level (Modern Portfolio Theory). |
| **MPT** | Modern Portfolio Theory | Framework for building portfolios optimising return vs risk (Markowitz). |
| **Rebalancing** | — | Restoring target weights by trimming winners / adding to laggards. |
| **Risk Appetite / Tolerance** | — | How much loss an investor can accept. |
| **Risk-Free Rate** | Rf | Return on a "safe" asset (e.g. T-bill / G-Sec); baseline for risk metrics. |
| **Robo-Advisor** | — | Automated, algorithm-driven portfolio management. |
| **Sharpe-optimal / Tangency Portfolio** | — | Portfolio on the efficient frontier with the highest Sharpe ratio. |
| **TWR / MWR** | Time-/Money-Weighted Return | TWR removes the effect of cash-flow timing (rates the manager); MWR = IRR (rates the investor's actual experience). |

## 13. Macroeconomics & Indicators

| Term / Abbr | Full form | Definition |

| **CPI** | Consumer Price Index | Measure of retail inflation. |
